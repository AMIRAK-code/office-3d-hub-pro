# Office 3D Hub — 30-second ads: storyboard

Both rendered drafts (`out/*.mp4`) follow these boards exactly. Hand this to an editor or agency for a
polished version (voice-over, sound design, colour grade). Voice-over lines are optional; the drafts carry
the message in captions alone, so they work with sound off (as most social feeds play).

## Production notes
- Everything shown is the real product UI: no mock-ups. The cast is fictional, the avatars are emoji, and no customer or employee data appears.
- The music is the product's own synthesized lo-fi jukebox, so there is nothing to license.
- Safe zones: captions keep clear of the platform UI (bottom 15% and top 10% on vertical feeds).
- To re-render after copy changes, edit `page/config.js` or the scene files, then run `npm run render`.
